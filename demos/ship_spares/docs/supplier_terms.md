# Approved Supplier Terms - Fleet Procurement

Meridian Marine Services, Procurement document PR-ST-11, updated February 2025. Prices in the catalogue are in USD
and exclude delivery and taxes.

## Konkan Marine Spares (Mumbai)

- Standard lead time: 10 working days for in-stock items; 6-8 weeks for items with zero stock (made to order).
- Volume discount: 5% on orders above USD 5,000; 8% on orders above USD 15,000 (calculated on the parts value,
  before delivery).
- Delivery: free to Mumbai port; USD 180 per consignment to Kandla or Kochi.
- Payment terms: 45 days from invoice.

## Nordline Supply (Singapore)

- Standard lead time: 14 calendar days to an Indian port; express 5 calendar days at a 12% surcharge on the
  parts value.
- No volume discount, but filters and gaskets are sold only in multiples of the minimum order quantity.
- Delivery: USD 350 flat per consignment to any Indian port; free above USD 10,000 parts value.
- Payment terms: 30 days from invoice.

## Kochi Harbour Traders (Kochi)

- Standard lead time: 7 working days; safety items (SF- part numbers) ship within 3 working days.
- Volume discount: 3% on orders above USD 3,000.
- Delivery: free within Kochi port limits; USD 120 per consignment to other Indian ports.
- Payment terms: 30 days from invoice.

## General rules

1. Always order at least the minimum order quantity listed in the catalogue.
2. Where two suppliers list an equivalent part, prefer the one with the shorter lead time if the vessel is within
   30 days of a planned port call.
3. Old part references (for example "CP-77") must be cross-checked against the current MMS part number before
   ordering.
