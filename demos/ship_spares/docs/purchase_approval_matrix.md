PURCHASE APPROVAL MATRIX - SPARES AND STORES

Document PR-AM-03, effective 1 January 2025.

1. Approval limits (total order value in USD, after discounts, including delivery)

- Up to USD 2,500: Fleet Procurement Officer.
- USD 2,500 to 10,000: Fleet Procurement Manager.
- USD 10,000 to 50,000: Technical Director.
- Above USD 50,000: Chief Executive Officer, with two competing quotations attached.

2. Emergency purchases

When a vessel is off-hire or has a class condition, the Superintendent may approve up to USD 15,000 by email,
with regularisation in the purchase system within 2 working days.

3. Quotations

Orders above USD 5,000 need at least two quotations unless the part is maker-specific (OEM only).

4. Purchase orders

A purchase order number must be issued before the supplier ships. Orders without a PO number will not be paid.

5. Records

All purchase orders and quotations are kept for 7 years.
