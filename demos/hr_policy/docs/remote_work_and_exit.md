# Meridian Marine Services - Remote Work, Probation and Separation Rules

Document HR-RW-02, effective 1 January 2025.

## A. Work from home (WFH)

A1. Shore staff may work from home up to 2 days per week after completing probation, with line-manager approval.

A2. WFH is not available to Technical Superintendents during a vessel's dry-docking period or to Marine
Operations staff on the 24x7 duty roster.

A3. Employees working from home must be reachable on Teams between 10:00 and 17:00 IST and must attend in person
for the monthly all-hands meeting.

A4. A one-time home-office allowance of INR 15,000 is reimbursed for a chair or monitor, against a receipt, once
every 3 years.

A5. Working from outside India requires prior written approval from HR and the Head of Department, and is limited
to 20 working days per calendar year.

## B. Probation

B1. New permanent employees serve a probation period of 6 months. It may be extended once by up to 3 months.

B2. During probation the notice period is 15 days on either side.

## C. Notice periods (after confirmation)

C1. Staff up to Assistant Manager grade: 30 days.

C2. Managers and Senior Managers: 60 days.

C3. General Managers and above: 90 days.

C4. Notice may be shortened by mutual agreement; otherwise salary in lieu of the unserved notice is recovered
from the final settlement. Unused annual leave may NOT be adjusted against the notice period.

C5. The full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day.

## D. Exit process

D1. Resignation is submitted through the HR portal; the notice period starts on the day the resignation is
accepted by the line manager.

D2. An exit interview with HR is mandatory. Company laptops and access cards must be returned on the last day.
