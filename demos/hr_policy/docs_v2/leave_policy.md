# Meridian Marine Services - Leave Policy (Shore Staff)

Document HR-LP-04, effective 1 April 2026 (revision 2). Applies to all permanent shore-based employees in Mumbai and Kochi
offices. Sea-going staff are covered by the separate Crew Leave Rules.

## 1. Annual leave

1.1 Permanent employees earn 24 working days of annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per completed
month of service.

1.2 Employees in their first year may take annual leave only after completing 3 months of service.

1.3 Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward days must be used by
30 June of that year or they lapse.

1.4 Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days must be applied for at least 15 calendar days in advance through
the HR portal and approved by the line manager.

1.5 Leave encashment: on resignation, unused annual leave up to a maximum of 30 days is paid out at basic salary.

## 2. Sick leave

2.1 Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward.

2.2 A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave longer than 2
consecutive days.

2.3 Sick leave cannot be combined with annual leave to extend a holiday without HR approval.

## 3. Parental leave

3.1 Maternity leave: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third child
onwards. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.

3.2 Paternity leave: 15 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption.

3.3 Adoption leave: 12 weeks for an adoptive mother of a child under 3 months old.

3.4 Employees must notify HR in writing at least 8 weeks before planned parental leave begins, except in medical
emergencies.

## 4. Public holidays

4.1 The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published by HR every December.

4.2 Staff required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## 5. Contact

Questions not covered by this policy should be sent to the HR Business Partner at hr.helpdesk@meridian-marine.example
or raised as a ticket on the HR portal. Note: the older 2022 policy allowing 15 carry-forward days no longer applies.
