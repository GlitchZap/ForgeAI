TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Meridian Marine Services, document FIN-TE-07, revised March 2025. Applies to shore staff travelling on company
business, including ship visits and dry-dock attendance.

1. APPROVAL

1.1 All business travel must be approved in advance by the line manager. International travel also needs approval
from the Head of Department.

1.2 Travel requests must be raised at least 7 days before departure for domestic trips and 21 days before
international trips, except for urgent vessel attendance.

2. AIR AND RAIL TRAVEL

2.1 Economy class for all flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or more for
General Managers and above.

2.2 AC 2-tier is the standard for rail travel.

3. HOTELS AND DAILY ALLOWANCE

3.1 Hotel limits per night (domestic): metro cities INR 7,500; other cities INR 5,000.

3.2 Daily allowance for meals and local transport: INR 2,000 per day domestic, USD 60 per day international.
No receipts are needed for the daily allowance.

3.3 When the employee stays on board a vessel, the daily allowance is reduced by 50%.

4. CLAIMS

4.1 Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of returning from the trip.
Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected.

4.2 Original receipts are required for every item above INR 500, except the daily allowance.

4.3 Claims up to INR 25,000 are approved by the line manager. Claims above INR 25,000 also need Finance Controller
approval.

4.4 Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

5. NOT REIMBURSABLE

Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, laundry for trips shorter than 4 nights, and upgrades not covered
by section 2.

6. CONTACT

Questions on claims: finance.travel@meridian-marine.example.
