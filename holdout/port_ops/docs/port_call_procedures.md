# Port Call Procedures - India West and South Coast

Meridian Marine Services Operations, document OPS-PC-05.

## 1. Pre-arrival

1.1 The agent must receive the Pre-Arrival Notice (PAN) at least 96 hours before the ETA for Mumbai (JNPA) and
72 hours for Kochi and Mangaluru.

1.2 The crew list, ISPS declaration and last 10 ports of call must be attached to the PAN.

1.3 An ETA change of more than 4 hours must be re-notified to the agent and the port.

## 2. Pilotage and berthing

2.1 Pilotage is compulsory for all vessels above 200 GT.

2.2 Night berthing at Kochi is allowed only for vessels with a length overall below 200 m.

2.3 The vessel must have both anchors ready and the pilot ladder rigged 1.5 m above the water.

## 3. Weather limits for cargo operations

3.1 Container and bulk cargo operations are suspended when sustained wind speed exceeds 45 km/h (about 25 knots).

3.2 Bulk cargo operations of fertiliser, grain and cement are stopped during any rain.

3.3 Mooring lines must be doubled when the forecast wind exceeds 55 km/h.

3.4 During the south-west monsoon (1 June to 31 August), smaller ports may close; check with the agent daily.
