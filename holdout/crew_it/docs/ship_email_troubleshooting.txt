SHIP EMAIL TROUBLESHOOTING (MMS Mail client)

Ship email does not sync in real time. The vessel synchronises with the shore mail server every 2 hours, and
immediately when the Master presses "Send/Receive all" on the bridge PC.

If mail is not arriving:
1. Check the satellite link status light on the bridge PC. Amber or red means no link; wait until it is green.
2. Check the outbox. Messages larger than 5 MB are held and must be approved by the Master before they are sent.
3. Restart the MMS Mail client. Do not reinstall it.
4. If mail has not synced for more than 6 hours with a green link, the Master emails itsupport@meridian-marine.example
   or calls the 24-hour IT desk on the Fleet Broadband phone, extension 7.

Attachments: maximum 10 MB per message. Compress photos before sending.
Personal email (Gmail, Outlook.com) can be used on MMS-CREW from personal devices only.
